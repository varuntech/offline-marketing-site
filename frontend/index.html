Strategy

Why Offline Works

For trial-based products, physical activations deliver measurable advantages over digital-only acquisition.

Lower CAC for Trial Products

Immediate product experience drives higher conversion than ads. For beverage, food, and personal care brands, taste and touch matter.

First-Party Data Ownership

Capture email, SMS, and purchase intent directly. No platform intermediaries. You own the relationship from day one.

Real Customer Feedback

Face-to-face interactions reveal product positioning, messaging gaps, and feature preferences before scaling spend.

Strong Brand Trust & Recall

In-person experiences create memory structures digital ads can't replicate. Customers remember where they discovered you.
